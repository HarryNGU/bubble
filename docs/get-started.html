Get started

Set up Bubble in two minutes

Install it, say hello, and Bubble walks you through the rest. Everything after installing is optional.

1. Install

  1. Download Bubble

    From the download button. Your browser saves a file called Bubble-1.1.0.dmg.

  2. Drag Bubble into Applications

    Open the downloaded file and drag the Bubble icon onto the Applications folder.

  3. Open it

    Open Bubble from Applications.

    Bubble is signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens like any other app.

    The first time, macOS asks you to confirm: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway next to Bubble. You only do this once.

Want to check your download is genuine?

Open Terminal and run shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/Bubble-*.dmg. It should match this fingerprint exactly:

2. The welcome tour

The first time Bubble opens, it introduces itself in a short chat. It asks what to call you (optional, and kept on your Mac), lets you pick what you'd like help with, then asks for each permission one at a time, saying why before macOS shows its own prompt. Skip any you like; Bubble asks again only when you use that feature.

PermissionWhy Bubble asksNeeded for
Screen RecordingTo record your screen, to take a screenshot when you ask Bubble about it, and to hear your Mac's sound when you turn on Listen.Record, Ask, Listen
CameraFor the round camera bubble in recordings. Never used otherwise.Record (optional)
MicrophoneYour voice in recordings, dictation and when you talk to Bubble.Record, Dictate, Talk
Speech RecognitionTurning speech into text on your Mac.Dictate, Ask Bubble, captions
AccessibilityTo notice the ⌥⌥ double-tap in other apps and type text where your cursor is.Dictate, Ask Bubble messages

After allowing Screen Recording, macOS may ask you to quit and reopen Bubble. Your choices are saved. You can review or remove any permission later in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

3. Connect your AI (optional)

Ask Bubble needs an AI to answer. The tour asks which you'd like, or you can skip it:

  • Claude Code on your Mac (recommended). If you already use Claude Code, Ask Bubble uses it with your own Claude plan. It brings your memory notes and your connected apps (like Gmail and Calendar). Sign in once by opening Terminal, running claude and typing /login.
  • An Anthropic API key. Create one at console.anthropic.com and paste it into Bubble. It's stored in your Mac's Keychain and billed to your Anthropic account.
  • Not now. Recording and dictation work without any AI.

Other assistants such as ChatGPT or Gemini can't be connected yet.

Before Ask Bubble can see or hear anything, it shows you its ground rules and asks you to agree. You can read them on the safety page.

4. Sharper voice and captions (optional)

Whisper is a free speech model that runs on your Mac and is much more accurate than the built-in one. To use it, install Homebrew, run brew install whisper-cpp in Terminal, then download the model from Bubble's welcome tour or reel editor (574 MB, once). Nothing is uploaded.

5. Shortcuts

KeysWhat it does
⌥⇧RStart a recording (pick what to record), or finish one
⌥⇧ADraw an area, then record it
⌥ ⌥Double-tap to dictate; it finishes when you pause (or tap ⌥ once)
⌥\Show or hide Ask Bubble
⌥⇧\Talk to Ask Bubble
EscCancel picking, the countdown, dictation, or hide Ask Bubble

Click any shortcut inside Bubble to change it.

Uninstall

Bubble keeps nothing outside your Mac, so removing it is complete:

  1. Quit Bubble and drag it from Applications to the Bin.
  2. Optional: delete its data folder, ~/Library/Application Support/Bubble, and its log, ~/Library/Logs/Bubble.log. Your recordings in ~/Desktop/Bubble Recordings are yours to keep or delete.
  3. Optional: if you saved an API key, open the Keychain Access app, search for "Bubble AI polish" and delete it.
  4. Optional: remove Bubble from System Settings → Privacy & Security lists (Screen Recording, Microphone and so on).
