What it does

One assistant for everything on your Mac.

01 · Ask

Ask about anything on your screen

Stuck on a question in your Notion notes, a chart in Excel or a paragraph in a PDF? Press ⌥\ and ask. Bubble looks at your screen and answers like a friend texting back, with pictures, videos and links when they help.

  • Looks at your screen only when you ask
  • Diagrams, molecules and YouTube videos right in the chat
  • Works over every app, nothing to copy and paste
⌥\ to open
02 · Knows you

Not a stranger

Bubble picks up where your AI already is. Connect Claude and it knows your notes, your calendar and your inbox. Use ChatGPT? Bring over what it remembers about you in one paste.

  • Uses your Claude memory and connected apps
  • Imports what ChatGPT knows about you
  • Kept on your Mac, and you can delete it any time
03 · Do

Done in Gmail, sent by you

“Reply to Sam and say I'll be there at 6.” Bubble writes the reply in Gmail or Outlook, shows it to you, and waits. It only sends when you tap Allow, or say “yes”.

  • Drafts in Gmail, Outlook, Messages, Instagram and Messenger
  • An Allow or Deny card before anything is sent
  • Messages are typed in for you to send yourself
04 · Dictate

Talk instead of typing

Double-tap Option in iMessage, Slack, Word or anywhere else, and just speak. Bubble types a tidy version where your cursor is, and finishes on its own when you pause.

  • Works in every text box on your Mac
  • No ums, and your corrections applied
  • Speech is turned into text on your Mac
⌥⌥ double-tap
05 · Listen

A second brain on Zoom

Turn on Listen in a Zoom or Google Meet call, a lecture or a YouTube video. Bubble hears your Mac's sound (never your microphone) and tells you what to say, or sums up what you missed.

  • “What should I say?” in one tap
  • Summaries and to-dos from long calls
  • A red dot shows whenever it's listening
06 · Record

Record once, post everywhere

Hover any window and click. Your face sits in a round bubble, and the noise is gone. One more click makes captioned versions for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and YouTube.

  • Pauses cut out automatically
  • Big captions timed to your words
  • 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9, ready to post
⌘\ to record

Questions

Good to know.

Is Bubble really free?

Yes. It's open source with no paid tier and no ads. The assistant uses an AI you bring yourself (your Claude plan or your own API key).

Can Bubble do anything without me knowing?

No. It only looks, listens or acts when you ask, shows a red dot while it's listening, and anything that sends or changes something waits for your Allow.

I use ChatGPT. Does Bubble know what it knows about me?

You can bring it over: Bubble gives you a short prompt to paste into ChatGPT, and you paste the answer back. It's saved on your Mac and you can delete it any time. The answers themselves come from Claude.

Which Macs does it work on?

Any Mac on macOS 15 Sequoia or later, with Apple silicon or Intel. There's no Windows or phone version yet.
