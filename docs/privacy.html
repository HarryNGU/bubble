Privacy policy

What Bubble does with your information: nothing is collected.

Last updated 22 October 2026

Bubble is a free, open-source Mac app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app and this website. The short version: we don't collect anything, and Bubble only sends information to other services when you use a feature that needs them.

Who we are

Bubble is an independent open-source project published at github.com/HarryNGU/bubble. There's no company account system and no servers run by Bubble. Contact us through GitHub issues.

What we collect

Nothing. Bubble has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking. We don't receive your recordings, voice, screenshots, messages, contacts or settings, and we couldn't see them if we wanted to.

What stays on your Mac

  • Recordings and reels (~/Desktop/Bubble Recordings).
  • Voice: dictation, captions and Ask Bubble's listening are transcribed on your Mac. Audio is never uploaded.
  • The name you asked Bubble to call you, if you gave one. It is only shown to you.
  • What you brought over from another assistant about yourself, if you did (in ~/Library/Application Support/Bubble/about-me.md, readable only by your user account). You can delete it in Bubble's settings.
  • Settings, snippets, transcripts of your recordings, and Ask Bubble's latest screenshot (in ~/Library/Application Support/Bubble, readable only by your user account).
  • Your Anthropic API key, if you add one, in the macOS Keychain.
  • A log file for troubleshooting (~/Library/Logs/Bubble.log). It stays on your Mac unless you choose to share it.

When information leaves your Mac

Only when you use these features, and only to these services:

Those services handle the information under their own privacy policies, including Anthropic's. Bubble doesn't sell or share your information with anyone else, ever.

Other people's information

If you turn on Listen during a call, what other people say is transcribed on your Mac and may be included when you ask a question. Tell people before you do this. In some places the law requires their consent.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or third-party scripts. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log your IP address for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. Downloads come from GitHub.

Children

Bubble isn't meant for children under 13. Features that use Anthropic require you to meet Anthropic's age requirements, which are 18 or older.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold any of your information, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your Mac: you can delete recordings, clear Bubble's data folder, remove your API key and revoke permissions in System Settings at any time (see Uninstall). For information you sent to Anthropic or another service, use that service's privacy tools.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above, and note the change in the release notes. Bubble's code is public, so any change in what it does is visible.
