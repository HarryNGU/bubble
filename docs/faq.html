FAQ

Questions people ask

Can't find yours? Ask on GitHub and we'll help.

Is Bubble really free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT licence. There's no paid tier and no ads. Ask Bubble uses an AI you bring yourself (your Claude plan or your own API key), and that provider may charge you for it.

Which devices does it work on?

Macs running macOS 15 Sequoia or later, with Apple silicon or Intel. There's no Windows, Linux, iPhone or Android version.

Is it safe to download?

Bubble is open source, so anyone can read exactly what it does, and every release lists a SHA-256 fingerprint so you can confirm the file hasn't been changed (see the install guide). Only download Bubble from this site or its GitHub page.

What does Bubble collect about me?

Nothing. There's no account, no analytics and no tracking. The only time anything leaves your Mac is when you use a feature that needs the internet, like asking Ask Bubble a question, and the privacy policy lists exactly what is sent and to whom.

Does Bubble record me all the time?

No. It records only when you start a recording, hears your voice only while you dictate or talk to it, hears your Mac's sound only while Listen is on (a red dot shows), and takes a screenshot only when you ask it something.

What does Listen hear?

Only the sound playing on your Mac: the other people on a call or meeting, or a video you're watching. It doesn't use your microphone. To talk to Bubble yourself, use Talk (⌥⇧\) or dictation.

Can Ask Bubble send messages or emails for me?

It can prepare them, but it never sends on its own. Emails need you to approve them. For Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp and Messages it opens the chat and types the message, and you press Enter.

Can I use it with ChatGPT or another AI?

Not yet. Ask Bubble works with Claude, either through Claude Code on your Mac or with an Anthropic API key. Recording and dictation don't need any AI.

Does it know my previous Claude chats?

Through Claude Code it knows your Claude Code memory notes and connected apps. It can't read your chat history from the Claude app or website, because Claude doesn't let other apps do that.

Can people tell I'm using Ask Bubble on a call?

By default, yes: if you share your screen, Bubble's chat shows up in the share and in recordings like any other window. If your chat is private, one switch hides it from every recording and screen share, Bubble's own and Zoom, Meet or Teams alike. Please don't use that to cheat in exams or interviews, and don't record people without telling them.

Where are my recordings?

In Bubble Recordings on your Desktop, and in Bubble's library. Reels are saved in its Reels folder. Nothing is uploaded.

Dictation didn't type into my app. Why?

Bubble needs Accessibility permission to type into other apps (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility). Without it, your words are copied so you can paste them. Some secure fields, like password boxes, block typing on purpose.

How do I remove Bubble completely?

See Uninstall. Because Bubble has no servers, deleting it from your Mac removes it completely.

How do I report a bug or a security problem?

Bugs: open an issue on GitHub. Security problems: please report them privately through GitHub's private security reporting, not in a public issue.
